# Privacy Policy for ScaleSmart

**Last Updated: May 02, 2026**

At ScaleSmart, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our digital solutions, systems design services, and recruitment platform.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that helps us provide a better experience and service to you:

- **Contact Data**: Name, email address, WhatsApp number, and social media handles (LinkedIn, Facebook) when you reach out via our contact forms.
- **Service Data**: Information about your business, tools, and operational needs provided during strategy sessions.
- **Career Data**: For VA candidates, we collect CVs, portfolios, work experience, tool proficiency, and skills as part of our application process.
- **Usage Data**: Technical information such as IP addresses, browser type, and how you interact with our website to improve performance.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

ScaleSmart uses your data for the following purposes:

- **Service Delivery**: To design and implement your custom operational systems.
- **Communication**: To respond to inquiries, send system logs, and coordinate strategy calls.
- **Recruitment**: To evaluate and contact candidates for Virtual Assistant roles.
- **System Improvement**: To analyze how our tools (like the Prompt Generator) are used and optimize them.

## 3. Data Protection and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. As a systems-first agency, we prioritize technical security in every workflow we build.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal data to third parties. We only share information with:

- **Internal Team**: Vetted operators and managers within ScaleSmart who need the data to perform their roles.
- **Service Providers**: Trusted tools (like Google Workspace or automation platforms) that are essential for our operations.
- **Legal Compliance**: When required by law to protect our rights or comply with a judicial proceeding.

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Request corrections to any inaccurate information.
- Request the deletion of your data from our active systems.
- Opt-out of any marketing communications.

I prioritize transparency and the security of your data. This policy outlines how I collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with this portfolio and the tools hosted here, such as the **Prompt Request Generator**.

## 1. Data Collection & Usage

I only collect information that is essential for providing the services and improving your experience.

### Personal Data

When you interact with the platforms, I may collect:

- **Contact Information**: Email address and name for communication if you choose to reach out.
- **Tool-Specific Data**: Information required for specific tools to function correctly.

### Usage Data

Diagnostic data is automatically collected to ensure system stability and performance:

- IP addresses, browser types, and device identifiers.
- Interaction logs with tools like the **Prompt Request Generator**.

## 2. How I Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for functional and operational purposes:

- **Service Delivery**: Providing the tools and features you interact with.
- **Communication**: Responding to inquiries or providing updates if requested.
- **System Optimization**: Analyzing usage patterns to improve tool performance and reliability.

## 3. Data Sovereignty & Security

I believe in **Data Sovereignty**. Your information is handled with high security standards:

- I do not sell your personal data to third parties.
- I use industry-standard encryption to protect data in transit and at rest.
- I only share data with trusted service providers (e.g., Supabase, Vercel) necessary for the infrastructure of this site.

## 4. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access the personal data I hold about you.
- Request the correction or deletion of your information.
- Opt-out of any communications at any time.

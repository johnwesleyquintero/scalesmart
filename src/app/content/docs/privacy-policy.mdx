## Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 21, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use this application.

### Data Storage

Currently, your data is stored locally within your web browser's IndexedDB. This is part of our free tier service. We do not collect or store any personal data on our servers at this time.

### Data Usage

The stored data is used solely to provide the application's core functionality and improve your user experience. We do not share your data with any third parties.

### User Rights

You have the right to access, modify, and delete your data. You can do this by clearing your browser's site data. We also encourage you to use any available "Export" features to back up your data.

### Future Plans

We are planning to introduce a paid tier with cloud storage. We will update this policy to reflect any changes in data handling practices when this feature is available.

### Third-Party Services

For the core functionality of this application, we do not utilize third-party services that collect your personal data.

We use Vercel Analytics to understand general traffic patterns and improve our website experience. Vercel Analytics collects anonymized and aggregated data, including metrics such as page views, visit durations, browser types, operating systems, and general geographic locations (derived from IP addresses but anonymized). This data helps us understand how users interact with our site and identify areas for improvement. For detailed information on Vercel's privacy practices regarding their analytics, please visit their [Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy). Vercel Analytics is designed to be privacy-focused and collects anonymized, aggregated data; it does not track individual users or use cookies for tracking purposes.

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be announced prominently within the application and via email to registered users, ensuring you are always informed of how your information is handled.

For any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@scalesmart.com.

# Amazon Keyword Research System: Amazon Autocomplete + SEMrush Workflow

---

## System Overview

Keyword research in Amazon SEO is not about guessing keywords.

It is about extracting **real user intent signals** from two sources:

- Amazon search autocomplete (behavior-based intent)
- SEMrush search volume (external demand validation)

This system combines both into a structured workflow for listing optimization and product research.

---

## Core Principle

> Keywords are not created. They are extracted from behavior.

Amazon autocomplete reflects **buyer intent inside Amazon**.

SEMrush reflects **demand outside Amazon (Google ecosystem)**.

Together, they form a validated keyword set.

---

## Phase 1 — Amazon Autocomplete Extraction

### Step 1: Identify Seed Keyword

Start with a base product or category term:

Example:

- RC cars
- bath

These act as **intent anchors**, not final keywords.

---

### Step 2: Collect Amazon Suggestions

Go to Amazon search bar and type the seed keyword.

Extract autocomplete suggestions such as:

For “RC cars”:

- rc cars for adults
- rc cars for kids
- rc cars fast
- rc cars off road

For “bath”:

- bath towels
- bath bombs
- bath mat
- bath accessories

---

### Step 3: Structure the Data

Organize into a simple table:

```
| Keyword  | Search Term |
|----------|-------------|
| RC cars  | rc cars for adults |
| RC cars  | rc cars for kids   |
```

No extra fields or enrichment at this stage.

---

## Phase 2 — SEMrush Validation Layer

### Step 4: Search Each Keyword

Take each extracted search term and input it into SEMrush.

Focus on:

- Google US search volume

This adds an external validation layer to Amazon intent.

---

### Step 5: Map Search Volume

Add a third column:

```
| Keyword | Search Term | Impressions |
|---------|-------------|-------------|
| RC cars | rc cars for adults | 5400 |
| bath    | bath towels        | 27100 |
```

This creates a hybrid dataset:

> Amazon intent + Google demand signal

---

## Phase 3 — Data Structuring

### Step 6: Sorting by Demand

Sort the dataset:

- Highest impressions → lowest impressions

This helps identify:

- high demand keywords
- mid-tier conversion keywords
- low-signal terms

---

## Core Insight Layer

This system is not about keyword volume alone.

It identifies three layers of intent:

### 🟢 High Demand + High Amazon Intent

Best for primary listing keywords

### 🟡 Moderate Demand

Best for secondary or long-tail keywords

### 🔴 Low Demand

Usually discard or deprioritize

---

## System Behavior Principle

> Amazon autocomplete defines relevance  
> SEMrush defines opportunity

Neither is enough alone.

---

## Execution Rule

Keep the process strict:

- no keyword clustering
- no external SEO tools beyond SEMrush
- no subjective rewriting of keywords
- no over-optimization during collection phase

The goal is **data integrity first, optimization second**

---

## Final Output Structure

A clean dataset should always end in:

| Keyword | Search Term | Impressions |

Sorted from highest to lowest.

This becomes the base layer for:

- Amazon listing optimization
- PPC keyword structuring
- SEO indexing strategy

---

## Core Philosophy

Good keyword research is not creative work.

It is **signal extraction from real user behavior systems**.

The operator’s job is to reduce noise, not add intelligence too early.

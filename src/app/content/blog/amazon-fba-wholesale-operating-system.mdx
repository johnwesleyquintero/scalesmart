# Amazon FBA Wholesale Operating Manual: Scan → Filter → Execute

## (Premise)

This document describes how an Amazon FBA Wholesale Virtual Assistant operates inside real execution environments.

It is not designed for interview preparation or theoretical explanation.

It is a **role simulation framework** for actual workflow execution.

Most operational failures in Amazon Wholesale come from:

* unstructured decision-making
* inconsistent workflow execution
* lack of clear processing systems for tasks

This manual solves that by defining a repeatable operating structure.

---

# Core Operating System: Scan → Filter → Execute

All work tasks are processed through this three-phase execution model.

---

## Step 1: Scan (Task Interpretation)

Before executing any task, the operator identifies the category of work:

* Product Sourcing → identifying viable wholesale products
* Product Analysis → evaluating ROI, demand, competition
* FBA Operations → managing shipments, PO creation, inbound tracking
* Account Management → Seller Central updates, listings, cases
* Supplier Coordination → communication and negotiation workflows

**Objective:** Understand what type of operational system the task belongs to before execution.

---

## Step 2: Filter (Execution Prioritization)

Once the task is identified, the operator filters execution focus:

* remove unnecessary complexity
* focus only on required output
* avoid non-essential analysis

Execution is guided by:

* relevance to business outcome
* clarity of instruction
* operational priority (inventory, revenue impact, urgency)

---

## Step 3: Execute (Work Output Structure)

All tasks are executed using a structured output format:

### 1. Direct Execution

Immediate action or decision applied to the task.

### 2. Supporting Logic

Brief explanation of how the decision was made.

### 3. Tools Used (if applicable)

Relevant tools or systems used during execution.

---

## Example Workflow

**Task: Evaluate a wholesale product opportunity**

### Direct Execution

The product is evaluated based on ROI, demand stability, and competition level before proceeding.

### Supporting Logic

Keepa and SellerAmp data are analyzed to ensure consistent sales trends and acceptable competition before sourcing.

### Tools Used

Keepa, SellerAmp SAS

# Execution Principles

## DO

* execute tasks with clarity
* follow structured workflows
* prioritize operational outcomes
* maintain accuracy in all data handling

## DON’T

* overanalyze beyond task scope
* introduce unnecessary theory
* deviate from required output
* complicate simple execution steps

---

# Operating Mindset

Every task is processed through one question:

> “What is the required output of this task, and what is the most direct way to achieve it?”

This ensures execution remains consistent and efficient.

---

# Role Definition

The operator functions as:

> A structured Amazon FBA Wholesale execution specialist responsible for sourcing, analysis, and operational workflows within Seller Central and supplier systems.

Focus is on reliability, accuracy, and consistency of execution.

---

# Closing Principle

> Operational clarity reduces system failure.
> Structured execution creates predictable outcomes.

This manual defines how work is executed, not how work is explained.

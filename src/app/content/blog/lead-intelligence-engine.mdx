# Lead Intelligence Engine v1: Scan → Score → Decide

## (Premise)

This system replaces subjective product sourcing with a structured scoring engine.

Instead of asking:

> “Is this a good product?”

We ask:

> “Does this product pass the system thresholds?”

---

# Core Principle

> “Leads are not evaluated emotionally. They are scored structurally.”

Every product is treated as a data object, not an opinion.

---

# System Flow: Scan → Score → Decide

---

## Step 1: Scan (Data Extraction Layer)

The operator collects raw product data:

- Price
- BSR
- Sales velocity
- Keepa history
- Offer count
- Buy Box behavior
- Weight
- Brand type
- IP risk signals

**Objective:** Convert product into structured evaluation input.

---

## Step 2: Score (Decision Engine Layer)

Each product is evaluated across five scoring dimensions.

---

### 1. Demand Score (0–5)

Measures sales consistency.

**Inputs:**

- Amazon monthly sales
- Keepa 12h / 30d sales trend
- BSR stability

**Rule:**

- High consistent sales = higher score

---

### 2. Profitability Score (0–5)

Measures financial viability.

**Inputs:**

- ROI %
- Net profit per unit
- Cost stability

**Thresholds:**

- &lt;10% ROI = 0–1
- 10–15% = 2–3
- 15–20% = 4
- 20%+ = 5

---

### 3. Competition Score (0–5)

Measures market saturation.

**Inputs:**

- Offer count
- Buy Box distribution
- Top seller dominance

**Rules:**

- Single dominant seller = low score
- Shared Buy Box = higher score

---

### 4. Stability Score (0–5)

Measures historical reliability.

**Inputs:**

- Keepa price chart consistency
- no spikes/dumps
- no sudden offer drops

**Red flag triggers:**

- volatile pricing
- erratic rank movement

---

### 5. Risk Score (0–5)

Measures Amazon and brand safety.

**Inputs:**

- IP complaints history
- gating risk
- brand restriction signals
- sudden offer disappearance

**Rule:**

- higher risk = lower score

---

# Step 3: Decision Logic

After scoring:

## Total Score Calculation

```

Total Score = Demand + Profitability + Competition + Stability + Risk
Max = 25

```

---

## Output Classification

### 🟢 Green Lead (18–25)

- Safe to execute
- Replenishable potential
- Low operational risk

---

### 🟡 Yellow Lead (13–17)

- Conditional opportunity
- Requires deeper validation
- Monitor before scaling

---

### 🔴 Red Lead (0–12)

- Reject immediately
- High risk or low return

---

# Execution Rules

## DO

- follow scoring system strictly
- prioritize data over intuition
- document reasoning briefly
- maintain consistency across leads

## DON’T

- override score based on “gut feeling”
- ignore risk signals for profit
- skip data validation steps
- manually adjust classification after scoring

---

# Operator Output Format

Every evaluated product must be documented as:

### Product Summary

- Name / Category
- Key metrics (BSR, ROI, offer count)

### Score Breakdown

- Demand: X/5
- Profitability: X/5
- Competition: X/5
- Stability: X/5
- Risk: X/5

### Final Decision

- Green / Yellow / Red

### Reasoning (1–2 lines max)

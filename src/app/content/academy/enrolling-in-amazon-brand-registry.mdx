Enrolling in Amazon Brand Registry requires brand owners to provide specific information and documentation to verify their brand ownership. This includes having an active registered trademark, providing details about the brand's products and packaging, and agreeing to Amazon's Brand Registry terms and conditions. The enrollment process can take several weeks to complete, but the benefits of Brand Registry make it a worthwhile investment for brand owners.

Reporting intellectual property infringements to Amazon is a crucial step in protecting your brand and maintaining a fair marketplace. Brand owners can use Amazon's reporting tools to submit complaints about counterfeit products, trademark violations, and other forms of infringement. Providing detailed information and evidence when reporting infringements can help Amazon take swift action against offenders and protect your brand's interests.

## Types of Infringements to Report

- **Counterfeit Products:** Report listings that offer fake or replica versions of your branded products.
  - Example: If you find a listing selling a product that looks identical to yours but is being sold at a significantly lower price and is of inferior quality, it is likely a counterfeit product.
- **Trademark Violations:** Report listings that use your brand name or logo without authorization.
  - Example: If you find a seller using your brand name in their product title or description without your permission, it is a trademark violation.
- **Copyright Infringements:** Report listings that use your copyrighted images, videos, or text without authorization.
  - Example: If you find a seller using your product images or descriptions without your permission, it is a copyright infringement.

## How to Report Infringements

- **Access the Brand Registry Reporting Tool:** Log in to your Brand Registry account and access the reporting tool.
- **Provide Detailed Information:** Provide detailed information about the infringement, including the ASIN of the infringing product, the seller's name, and a description of the infringement.
- **Submit Evidence:** Submit evidence to support your claim, such as your trademark registration certificate, photos of the counterfeit product, or screenshots of the unauthorized use of your copyrighted material.

## Following Up on Your Reports

- **Track Your Reports:** Track the status of your reports in the Brand Registry portal.
- **Provide Additional Information:** If Amazon requests additional information, provide it promptly.
- **Escalate Your Reports:** If Amazon does not take action on your reports, escalate them to a higher level of support.

**Objective:**
Audit the specified pages and components to identify opportunities for applying light and dark mode compatibility. Ensure all elements, including buttons, tabs, badges, and select menus, consistently use our brand colors while optimizing for accessibility.

**Scope:**
The audit will cover the following pages:

- `/` (Home Page)
- `/academy` (Academy Page)
- `/amazon-seller-tools` (Amazon Seller Tools Page)
- `/ats` (ATS Page)
- `/blog` (Blog Page)
- `/docs` (Documentation Page)
- `/chat` (Chat Page)
- `/crm` (CRM Page)
- `/project-management` (Project Management Page)
- `/workflow-builder` (Workflow Builder Page)

**Tasks:**

1. **Identify Elements:**

   - Locate all interactive and visual elements such as buttons, tabs, badges, and select menus.

2. **Check Compatibility:**

   - Assess the current compatibility of these elements with both light and dark modes.
   - Note any inconsistencies or accessibility issues.

3. **Apply Brand Colors:**

   - Ensure all elements use our brand colors consistently.
   - Adjust colors as necessary to meet accessibility standards and improve user experience.

4. **Consistency Review:**

   - Verify that the brand theme is uniform across all specified pages.
   - Document any deviations and suggest corrections.

5. **Accessibility Optimization:**
   - Make sure that color contrasts meet accessibility guidelines.
   - Adjust colors and styles to enhance readability and usability in both light and dark modes.

**Deliverables:**

- A detailed report of findings, including screenshots and descriptions of inconsistencies.
- Recommendations for adjustments to achieve consistency and improve accessibility.
- Updated style guidelines for implementing brand colors in light and dark modes.

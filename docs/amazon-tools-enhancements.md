# Amazon Seller Tools Enhancements

## Objective

Develop the "Amazon Seller Tools" feature to integrate key functionalities such as product research, keyword tracking, listing optimization, and analytics. This aims to enhance the tool's effectiveness and user experience for Amazon sellers.

## Suggested Features

Here's a plan for modern features to add to the Amazon Seller Tools Dashboard:

1.  **AI-Driven Product Opportunity Scoring:** An AI model that analyzes various factors (e.g., search volume, competition, pricing, seasonality) to identify promising product opportunities.
2.  **Intelligent Keyword Recommendations:** An AI-powered engine that suggests relevant and high-converting keywords based on product data, competitor analysis, and market trends.
3.  **Automated Listing Optimization:** AI algorithms that automatically optimize product titles, descriptions, and bullet points to improve search ranking and conversion rates.
4.  **Predictive Inventory Management:** AI models that forecast future demand based on historical sales data, seasonality, and market trends to optimize inventory levels and prevent stockouts or overstocking.
5.  **Automated Competitor Tracking:** Automatically track competitor pricing, sales, and listing changes to identify opportunities and threats.
6.  **AI-Powered Review Analysis:** Sentiment analysis of product reviews to identify key customer concerns and areas for improvement.
